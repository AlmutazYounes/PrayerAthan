# Hourly athkar sources

Short dhikr for the wall clock, on the local hour between Fajr and Isha, and only from 8:00 AM through 9:00 PM. Masters stay in this folder. The APK copies them to `app/src/main/res/raw/` as `athkar_*.mp3`.

Not Quran recitation. Personal use on Mutaz's wall. Same rule as the athan files: replace before strangers install from Play. Commons clips have a real CC license. The Archive.org items do not.
