# Places

`cities.tsv` and `countries.tsv` are GeoNames `cities15000` and `countryInfo`, CC BY 4.0.

https://www.geonames.org/
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/

Cities with about 15,000 people or more. Not every village. Timezones come with the dump, so picking a city does not need the network.
