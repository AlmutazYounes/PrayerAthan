# Privacy policy

PrayerAthan
Package `com.mutazyounes.prayerathan`

Publisher: Mutaz Younes
Play developer name: YounesM

Last updated: 27 August 2026

## Who publishes this app

Mutaz Younes publishes PrayerAthan. On Google Play the developer name is YounesM. There is no company server behind the app and no user accounts.

This page is the privacy policy for the Android app PrayerAthan.

## What the app is

PrayerAthan is a wall clock for Android tablets. It shows prayer times and plays the call to prayer at Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha. Sunrise is listed and silent.

Times are calculated on your tablet. The app does not download a timetable. It does not need the internet after a location is saved.

## Data that leaves the device

None, in the app we ship today.

The app does not create accounts. It does not include ads. It does not include analytics. It does not include a crash reporter that uploads stack traces. It does not send your data to Mutaz, to an ad network, or to a prayer-time API.

On Google Play, "collect" means data is transmitted off your device. PrayerAthan does not collect data in that sense.

## Location

The clock needs a latitude, longitude, timezone, and a city label so it can compute ISNA times.

The default is Albany, NY. Those coordinates are built into the app.

If you save a different city or coordinates, that value stays on the tablet. It is not uploaded. It is not shared with other users.

Today the app does not use GPS. It does not ask Android for the location permission. If a later version lets you tap once for a GPS fix, that fix is still stored on the device for prayer math only. The permission would be requested at that tap, not in the background.

Android backup may copy app data into your device backup if you have backups turned on. That is Android copying your tablet, not this app sending location to us.

## Alarms, notifications, and reboot

The app schedules exact alarms so athan can start at prayer time. While the file plays, Android shows a notification with Stop. After a reboot, the app reschedules those alarms. It does not start playback at boot.

Those permissions exist so the wall clock keeps working. They are not used to track you.

## Audio

Athan recordings are bundled in the app. They play on your tablet. They are not your voice. They are not uploaded.

## How to delete what is on the tablet

There is no account to close and no web form, because we never received your data.

Uninstall PrayerAthan, or clear the app's storage in Android settings. That removes any saved location on that device.

## Children

This app is a wall clock for the person who hangs the tablet. It is not directed at children.
