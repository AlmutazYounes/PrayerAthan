This listing is **free**. Mutaz 27 Aug 2026. Package `com.mutazyounes.prayerathan` cannot become paid. Official: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6334373

A later paid clock is a new `applicationId`. Do not build Play Billing, ads, IAP, a donation button, or accounts on this app.

The screen never sleeps. An ad on that wall is a billboard in a living room. AdMob also wrecks Data safety. Skip.

## Why IAP stays out

A buy button after Isha is the commercial app Mutaz is leaving. Play Billing would add purchase history to Data safety. Skip.
